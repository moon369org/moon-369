# MIP-0001: The lune

- **Author:** Moon
- **Status:** Adopted (v1, wording only)
- **Version affected:** v1 (wording only)

## Summary

Call the Moon day a **lune**. A lune is one 369th of a Moon year: 86,400 Moon seconds, or 89,888.23 SI seconds (about 24h 58m). It turns over at the same instant everywhere.

## Motivation

"Day" means sunrise to sunrise to almost everyone. Using it for the Moon calendar unit made people expect the date to change overnight where they live, when it actually changes at one global instant. Two different things shared one word.

## Specification

- 1 lune = 86,400 Moon seconds = 89,888.23 SI seconds.
- 369 lunes = 1 Moon year = 13 mean lunations.
- Each moon is 28 or 29 lunes.
- "Day" keeps its everyday meaning, the light: Light and Dark count it.

## Compatibility

Wording only. No result changes. API field names such as `day` and `days` stay as they are in v1.

## Alternatives

- **Phase:** already means the moon's shape, and Light or Dark in the API.
- **Tide:** a tidal day is 24h 50m, not 24h 58m.
- **Lunar day:** astronomers use it for the moon's own 29.5-day rotation.
