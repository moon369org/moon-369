CHECK MOON TIME AGAINST THE SKY

Moon's dates follow the real Moon, so you can check them by looking up. Take a photo of the Moon and Moon measures how much of it is lit and which way the lit side points, then checks both against what Moon time says the sky should show, right where you're standing.

WHY THIS WORKS

Every Moon month starts at the new moon, so the date tells you the phase. And the Moon's phase is the same for everyone on Earth at the same moment, unlike the Sun, which tells the time only where you stand. Which way the lit side tilts depends on your place in the world, so Moon works that out from your location. The sky can confirm the date to about a day; the nanoseconds and the proof come from Moon's signatures.
