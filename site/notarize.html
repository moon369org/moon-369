NOTARIZE · A TIMESTAMP FOR ANY DOCUMENT

NOTARIZE A DOCUMENT

Drop in a PDF, a contract or any file. Moon signs its fingerprint at this exact moment and gives you a certificate to print or save, with a QR code anyone can scan to check it. Your document never leaves your device.

Both optional. They are printed on the certificate only and are never sent to Moon.

WHAT THE CERTIFICATE PROVES

01 · THIS EXACT DOCUMENT

The fingerprint matches only this file. Change one character, one pixel or one byte and it no longer matches.

02 · AT THIS MOMENT

Moon signed it at the time shown, and a public drand beacon proves it could not have been signed any earlier.

03 · ANYONE CAN CHECK

Scan the QR code or drop the document into /check. The receipt also checks offline with Moon's public keys.

What it does not prove: who wrote the document, or that anyone signed or agreed to it. It is a cryptographic timestamp, not the act of a notary public. For contracts, sign as you normally would and notarize the signed copy, so the certificate shows when that final version existed. Moon's time-stamp authority also works inside PDF signing tools: RFC 3161 at /v1/tsa.
