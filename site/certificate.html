CERTIFICATES OF AUTHENTICITY

Drop in the master file of your artwork and fill in its details. Moon signs the file and the edition, and you print one certificate per piece. Collectors scan the QR code to see the signed details on their phone. Your file never leaves your device.

WHAT A COLLECTOR CAN CHECK

01 · THE DETAILS

Scanning the QR code re-checks the artist, title, year and edition against Moon's signature. Change one letter and it fails.

02 · THE MASTER FILE

Its fingerprint is on the certificate and was signed by Moon, so the original file can be matched exactly at /verify, and edited copies found as look-alikes.

03 · WHEN

Both signatures carry a Moonmark and a public drand beacon, so neither could have been made earlier than shown.

What it does not prove on its own: who the artist is. Names show as typed; Moon does not verify them. Print numbers are written on each certificate; one signature covers the whole edition.
