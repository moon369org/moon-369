TIDELINE

A permanent, public list of everything signed on Moon. Each item is listed the moment it's signed. When the lune ends, the list is sealed and chained to every lune before it. After that, no one can add, remove or backdate an item, Moon included.

    What people use it for

    You shot it first

    A photographer signs the original. If it's stolen later, the record shows whose was here first.

    This is the version we agreed to

    Sign a contract the day it's final. Any edited copy won't match the sealed one.

    Made by a person

    Sign your work as you make it, so its history shows it existed before anyone could have generated it.

    My idea, dated

    Inventors and writers sign drafts and notes to prove when an idea was theirs.

    How a tide is sealed

    01

    Written down

    Sign an item and its fingerprint is listed here instantly. The item itself stays with you.

    02

    Sealed

    When the lune ends, the whole list is sealed into one tide. Nothing can be slipped in or pulled out.

    03

    Chained

    Each tide is locked to the one before it, so today's seal protects every day in the past.

    04

    Checked by anyone

    Anyone can verify a receipt in their own browser, with no account and no need to trust Moon.

      Details for auditors

      Each tide is a Merkle tree of item fingerprints. Its root is signed with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65 and includes the previous tide's fingerprint, so every tide is chained to the one before it. The raw counts are at /v1/stats, and you can audit it yourself.
