TIDELINE

Every lune, Moon seals its signed Moonmarks into a tide: one fingerprint of every receipt, chained to the tide before it, signed with Moon's keys and anchored on the XRP Ledger and Bitcoin. While a lune is open, every receipt is locked on the XRP Ledger within about a minute. Once locked, no one can add, remove or backdate a receipt, Moon included.

01 · RECORD

Every receipt Moon signs is added to its lune's list the moment it's signed.

02 · SEAL

When the lune ends, its receipts become one Merkle root, the same structure that secures website certificates, signed with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65.

03 · CHAIN

Each tide includes the previous tide's fingerprint, so the newest tide vouches for the whole history.

04 · ANCHOR

Every receipt is locked on the XRP Ledger within about a minute, in a checkpoint. When the lune seals, its tide is locked on the XRP Ledger within seconds, and Bitcoin adds a second, independent lock within hours, so the record outlasts any single chain.
