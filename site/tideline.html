01 · RECORD

Every receipt Moon signs is added to its lune's list the moment it's signed.

02 · SEAL

When the lune ends, its receipts become one Merkle root, the same structure that secures website certificates, signed with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65.

03 · CHAIN

Each tide includes the previous tide's fingerprint, so the newest tide vouches for the whole history.

04 · ANCHOR

The tide's fingerprint is written to the XRP Ledger. Once the ledger closes, the tide can never be quietly changed.
