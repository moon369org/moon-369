# Moon brand statement

Moon (moon369.org) is the owner and author of the brand described here: the name, the marks and the
files listed in `moon-brand-manifest.json`. Every one of those files, this statement and the manifest
were fingerprinted with SHA-256 and signed by Moon with a Moonmark before they were published.

## The name

**Moon**, used for the time standard, its calendar, its timestamps (Moonmarks), its API and its website.

## The marks

- **Wordmark.** MOON in a wide geometric hand, with the two O's linked: one passes over the other at the
  top and under it at the bottom. Files in `logo/`.
- **Mark.** The two linked O's on their own. Files in `mark/`.
- **App icons.** The mark in a rounded square, dark and cream. Files in `app-icon/`.
- **Powered by Moon badge.** Files in `badges/`.
- **Loader.** One solid circle that breathes open into the two linked rings and closes again. Files in `loader/`.
- **Social images.** The X avatar, banner and "A new standard for time" image. Files in `social/`.

## Taglines

- The new standard for time
- Thirteen moons. One timestamp.

## Colors

Void #050506, Eclipse #0B0B0D, Graphite #16171A, Basalt #24262A, Ash #8A8B8F, Regolith #E9E4D8.
About 90 percent dark and 10 percent cream; Regolith is the only accent. Files in `colors/`.

## Type

Syncopate for display, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for data. These are free Google Fonts and are not
claimed by Moon; only their use together in Moon's identity is described here.
