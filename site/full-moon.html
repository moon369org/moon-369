      HOW EXACT

      A full moon is the instant the Moon sits exactly opposite the Sun. Moon computes it with the method almanacs use (Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, chapter 49) and checks it against PyEphem, a standard astronomy library: within 20 seconds for every full moon from 1900 to 2030. The Moon is rarely 100% lit even then, because it passes a little above or below the Earth's shadow line. Developers can get the same times from the API at /v1/full-moons.
