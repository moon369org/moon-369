03

HOW IT WORKS

No app to install, for members or the front desk. It all runs in the browser.

AN INVITE THAT WORKS ONCE

You send each member their own invite link. The first phone that opens it gets the pass, and the link is spent. Forward it and it does nothing.

THE PHONE MAKES A KEY

The member's phone makes a key pair and locks the private half inside its browser. Moon signs a pass bound to that key, with the membership's end date.

A NEW CODE EVERY 5 SECONDS

At the desk the phone shows a code only it can make. Screenshots go stale in 15 seconds, and a pass that just came in can't come in again for 30 minutes.

EVERY VISIT SIGNED

Moon signs each visit as its own Moonmark. You get an attendance record nobody can edit, and members get proof of every visit.

WHAT STOPS WHAT

A lent cardThere's no card. The pass is a key inside one phone.
A screenshotStale in 15 seconds. The next code needs the phone's key.
Handing the phone back outA pass that just came in can't come in again for 30 minutes.
A forwarded inviteEach invite works once. After that it says so.
A lapsed membershipIt stops on its end date. Renew it and the same pass works again.
No signal at the deskThe door checks passes itself and sends visits to Moon when it's back online.
