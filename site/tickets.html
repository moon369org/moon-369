03

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, and no app to install. It runs in the phone's browser.

THE PHONE MAKES A KEY

When a guest gets a ticket, their phone makes its own key pair. The private half is locked inside that browser: no one can copy it out, not even the guest.

MOON SIGNS THE TICKET

Moon signs a statement naming the event, the ticket and that phone's key. The ticket is a Moonmark, signed with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65 like every Moon receipt.

A NEW CODE EVERY 5 SECONDS

The QR carries the ticket, the current 5-second window and the phone's signature over both. The door accepts a code for 15 seconds, then it's worthless.

IN ONCE, SIGNED

The door checks the signature and lets each ticket in once. Moon signs the check-in, and it lands in the guest's Moonlog as proof they were there.

04

WHAT STOPS WHAT

A screenshotStale in 15 seconds. The next code needs the phone's key.
A forwarded linkThe key never left the first phone, so the second has nothing to sign with.
The same ticket twiceOne check-in per ticket. The second scan shows when it was used.
A fake ticketOnly Moon can sign one. The door knows every real ticket's key.
No signal at the doorThe door checks codes itself and sends check-ins to Moon when it's back online.
Resold ticketsA ticket can't move to another phone, so there's nothing to resell.
